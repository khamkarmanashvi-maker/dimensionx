In today’s competitive job market, students often apply for internships without clearly understanding the skills required by companies. Academic syllabus mainly focus on theory, while industries demand practical, tool-based, and job-ready skills. This gap results in low selection rates, lack of confidence, and wasted effort. Artificial Intelligence (AI) is redefining career navigation, empowering employees with personalized career coaching that helps them move up, across, and beyond within organizations. As workforce dynamics evolve, organizations must adopt AI-driven solutions to support internal mobility, talent fulfillment, reskilling, and workforce deployment. AI agents are emerging as intelligent career advisors that provide personalized recommendations, helping employees make informed career decisions while aligning their aspirations with organizational goals.

AI career advisors also facilitate internal mobility by identifying internal opportunities that employees might not be aware of. For example, an AI agent can:

• Match an employee’s skills with open internal positions, even those outside their current department.

• Suggest lateral moves that align with long-term career aspirations and organizational needs.

• Recommend personalized learning paths to help employees qualify for desired roles.

InternReady is an intelligent, student-centric platform that analyzes student profiles against real internship and job requirements, identifies missing competencies, and recommends personalized upskilling pathways. Move beyond generic career advice—create a scalable system that empowers students to make data-driven decisions and transition confidently from campus to career.
