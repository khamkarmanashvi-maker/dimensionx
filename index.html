DimensionX

In today’s competitive job market, students often apply for internships without clearly understanding the skills required by companies. Academic syllabi mainly focus on theory, while industries demand practical, tool-based, and job-ready skills. This gap results in low selection rates, lack of confidence, and wasted effort.

DimensionX is an intelligent, student-centric platform that analyzes student profiles against real internship and job requirements, identifies missing competencies, and recommends personalized upskilling pathways. Move beyond generic career advice—create a scalable system that empowers students to make data-driven decisions and transition confidently from campus to career.
